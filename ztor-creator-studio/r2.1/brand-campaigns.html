How brand campaigns work

Ztor signs the deal with the brand — once, centrally. You just opt in for a window you choose. No contract with the brand, no approval, and no exclusivity.

  1. A fan buys as normal in store and gets a reward code with their purchase.
  2. They scan it in the Ztor app. It credits you by default — they can switch to another creator they follow.
  3. The fan collects loyalty points, and your share of that purchase is released.
Example

A fan spends $10 at Starbucks and gets one code. They scan it, take 50 points, and $1 lands in your earnings.

The rate is set between Ztor and the brand, so it is the same for every creator and you cannot edit it. What you control is which brands you back and for how long.

A reward code is not a discount — the fan has already paid. It is proof of purchase that turns into points for them and a share for you.
