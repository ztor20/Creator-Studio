What is the creator’s vault?

A gated cloud drive that belongs to you. Upload media of any kind — stems, raw footage, photo dumps, unreleased mixes — and only fans in an eligible tier can open it.

Switching it on for a tier grants that tier access to the whole vault, not to individual files. Use the tier ladder to decide how deep into your archive someone gets.
