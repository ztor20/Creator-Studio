How a fan’s tier is decided
  1. Below the minimum loyalty points: always Fan.
  2. At or above it: the fan joins the ranking and is placed by rank — top 1% Inner Circle, top 10% Super Fans, top 30% Ranked Fans.
  3. At or above it but outside the top 30%: still Fan.

How the tier system behaves. These are product rules — surfaced here, owned upstream.

How a fan’s tier is decided
First the minimum loyalty points, then rank.
Top % must widen
Each lower tier must cover a larger top % than the one above it, or the settings won’t save. Exact validation logic pending upstream.
Versioned, never retroactive
Every save creates an immutable version. Revenue and badges already earned under an older version are never rewritten.
When fans move tiers
Points land within 5 seconds and fans move up the moment they qualify.
Seats per tier
Seats are rounded down, but Inner Circle always has at least 1.
When changes take effect
Gate changes apply at the next weekly recompute; scoring settings apply only to events after you save; benefits apply immediately.
