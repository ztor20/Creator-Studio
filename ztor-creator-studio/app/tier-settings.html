Rules & versioning

How the tier system behaves. These are product rules — surfaced here, owned upstream.

Dual-gate upgrade
A fan upgrades only when they clear both of the tier’s gates — the top % AND the loyalty-points floor.
Monotonic thresholds
Higher tiers must require strictly more than lower tiers, or the save is blocked. Exact validation — TBC, not invented here.
Versioned, non-retroactive
Each save creates an immutable tier config version. Past revenue and badges earned under an old version are not rewritten (mirrors §7.6 rate versioning). Versioning behaviour — TBC, owned upstream.
When fans move tiers
Points land within 5 seconds and fans move up the moment they qualify.
Seats per tier
Seats are rounded down, but Inner Circle always has at least 1.
When changes take effect
Threshold changes apply at the next weekly recompute. Scoring changes apply only to later events, and points already earned stay as they are. Benefit changes apply immediately.
