Platform Fee Settings

Three fees here: the payment fee (one rate site-wide), the currency swap fee (set per payment currency) and the platform fee (set per transaction type). Saving creates a new version that applies to future sales only — existing sales keep their frozen rate.

Payment fee

Each payment provider has its own rate.

The fixed amount is recorded in the platform base currency and converted at the prevailing rate when it applies to a creator.
