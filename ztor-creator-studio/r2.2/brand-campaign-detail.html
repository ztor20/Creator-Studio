Chosen vs defaulted

When a fan scans a reward code, the app pre-selects a creator for them — usually whoever they engage with most. They can change it.

So two very different things both arrive as “a scan”. One fan tapped past their default to name you. Another simply accepted the name already on screen. Only the first is evidence that your campaign persuaded anybody.

Example

Two campaigns both credit 500 scans. In one, 90% actively picked you — your promotion worked. In the other, 15% did — you were collecting scans you would have received anyway.

A high total with a low choice rate means the campaign is riding your existing audience, not growing it.
