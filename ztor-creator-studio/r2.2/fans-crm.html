How “Top %” works

A relative gate. It asks where a fan sits compared with everyone else — not how much they have.

  1. Every fan is ranked by loyalty points, highest first.
  2. Their rank becomes a percentile — rank ÷ total fans.
  3. “Top 10%” means the highest-scoring tenth of your audience.
Example

With 1,840 fans, Top 10% is the highest-ranked 184 of them.

Because it is relative, the bar moves as your audience grows. A fan can drop out of the top 10% without losing a single point — someone else simply overtook them.

Why there is also a points floor

An absolute gate. A percentile always produces a top 1% — even in a tiny audience. The floor is what stops a tier being handed out to someone who has not earned it.

Example

A creator with 40 fans: the top 1% is one person. If that fan has 120 points and Inner Circle’s floor is 800, they still do not enter. The tier stays empty until someone genuinely reaches it.

A fan must clear both gates. Being top 1% is not enough on its own, and neither is a high score outside the percentile.
